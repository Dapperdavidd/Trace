Questions

Plain answers.

Do I need to understand crypto?

No. Everything is written in plain words. If you can read “3 wallets that are usually right just bought this”, you can use trace.

Does trace hold my money?

No. trace is a self-custody wallet. Your keys stay on your device, and nobody at trace can move your money.

What is a smart wallet?

A wallet with a long record of good trades. trace only calls a wallet smart after 50 or more trades, and always shows you its win rate.

Is copying a wallet safe?

Copying always carries risk. That is why you can test with pretend money first, and why limits like a stop at minus 20% are on by default.

Which blockchains does it read?

Solana today. More networks are on the way.
